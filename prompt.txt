Current architecture note:

ros-amr-navigation is a navigation-only stack that consumes externally launched
slam_toolbox online SLAM/localization.

Runtime order:
1. Robot bringup publishes /scan, /tf, /tf_static, and odom/base TF.
2. slam_toolbox online mode publishes /map and map -> odom.
3. amr_bringup navigation.launch.py starts costmap, planning, local planning,
   motion control, BT navigation, recovery, and runtime observation.

The AMR navigation core does not launch a map server, does not launch
localization, and does not publish map -> odom.
